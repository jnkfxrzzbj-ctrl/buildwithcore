Starter: follow the hardware, one stage at a time

This is a Starter-specific companion to the ten-stage assembly checklist. The two drawings below are simplified teaching diagrams, not photographs, pinout maps or measured representations of the MSI board or Corsair power supply. Check each connector and mounting position against the exact manufacturer manual before touching the hardware. CORE has not physically assembled or tested this build. HOLD — do not buy parts yet.

01 / BEFORE YOU START

Identify the exact parts

Compare the model labels with the eight items above. Open the MSI B550M PRO-VDH WIFI manual and support page ↗ and the manuals supplied with your case and PSU.

Ready when: all models match and you can identify the motherboard's CPU socket, RAM slots, M2_1 and PCI_E1 from its diagrams.

Pause if: the board is a different variant or its BIOS support for the Ryzen 5 5600 is unknown.

02 / CPU + MEMORY

Match the RAM slot labels

Install the Ryzen 5 5600 only after confirming the socket orientation in the manual. For the Corsair 2×8GB DDR4 kit, use DIMMA2 and DIMMB2. Do not force the CPU or RAM.

Ready when: CPU retention is secure and both modules are fully seated.

Pause if: the CPU alignment mark, socket retention or RAM notch does not match.

03 / STORAGE + COOLER

Fit the SSD and boxed cooler

Use M2_1 for the Kingston NV3 SSD and the board's specified retention method. Fit the Ryzen boxed Wraith Stealth cooler according to its included mounting and thermal-interface instructions. Connect the fan to the CPU fan header.

Ready when: the SSD is retained, cooler mounting is secure and its fan lead is connected.

Pause if: a mounting piece, protective film, thermal paste or connector is unclear. Follow the exact supplied cooler instructions.

04 / CASE + POWER

Do not confuse CPU and GPU power

Install only the matching micro-ATX standoffs. Connect motherboard 24-pin ATX, CPU/EPS to CPU_PWR1, and the correct PCIe 6+2 lead to the Palit RTX 5060. Use the board's JFP1 diagram for case switches. These cable types are not interchangeable.

Ready when: connectors are identified from labels and manuals, fully seated without force and cables are clear of fans.

Pause if: any connector is unidentified or a cable does not fit easily.

05 / FIRST BOOT

Check before switching on

Check for loose screws, cable obstructions and connected fans. Plug the monitor into the graphics card, not the motherboard: the Ryzen 5 5600 has no integrated graphics. Initial startup can take time.

Ready when: BIOS recognises the CPU, 16GB RAM and SSD, and the CPU fan operates normally.

Pause if: there is smoke, a burning smell, electrical fault, stopped CPU fan or rapidly increasing CPU temperature. Power down safely.

06 / AFTER FIRST BOOT

Install Windows and learn the basics

Follow the Windows installation guidance, use official hardware drivers and confirm stable operation before trying A-XMP. Keep the care checklist for future maintenance.

Ready when: the OS recognises your hardware and the system remains stable during normal use.

Pause if: you cannot confidently identify the destination drive or the system overheats or crashes.
