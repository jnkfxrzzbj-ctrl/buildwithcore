THE CORE METHOD

THE SAME LOGIC.
EVERY TIME.

Our planned guides follow a simple sequence so you do the fiddly motherboard work while access is easiest, route power before the graphics card blocks your hands, and only close the case once the machine has successfully powered on.

01

Build the board

CPU, memory and SSD are installed outside the case where you can actually see what you're doing.

02

Prepare cooling

Air cooler or AIO hardware goes on before cable access becomes awkward.

03

Case + power

Motherboard, PSU and front-panel wiring are connected before the GPU arrives.

04

GPU + first boot

The final major component goes in, connections are checked, then you power on with the glass still off.
