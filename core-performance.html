03
Motherboard

MSI B850M GAMING PLUS WIFI6E

Micro-ATX AM5 board with Wi-Fi 6E, 2.5Gb Ethernet and two M.2 slots; useful gaming features without unnecessary expansion cost.

Model B850M GAMING PLUS WIFI6E

Exact model, price & compatibility

MSI B850M GAMING PLUS WIFI6E, micro-ATX. Fit only matching standoffs. RAM in A2/B2, SSD in M2_1, GPU in PCI_E2 (the long CPU-connected slot, not PCI_E1). CPU support starts at 7E81v1A0 for both 9600X and 9800X3D. Connect 24-pin ATX and the CPU EPS leads to the labelled power headers; JFP1 for case switches, JAUD1 for audio and matching USB headers per manual.
