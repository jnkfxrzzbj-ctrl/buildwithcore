## Compatibility and assembly
- **CPU/board:** AM5 Ryzen 9600X and MSI's stated Ryzen 9000 support align. Exact CPU-support minimum BIOS and the shipped BIOS have not been confirmed. Resolve before activation; document the appropriate update procedure. Do not confuse B850M WIFI6E with the ATX, MAX or back-connect models.
- **GPU power:** PowerColor specifies **two 8-pin PCIe sockets**, not the three sockets on Configuration C's ASUS Prime card. Corsair's 2025 RMe manual lists two separate PCIe 6+2 cables for RM850e. Connect one supplied lead to each socket. Do not use EPS/CPU leads or transplant modular leads from a different PSU. The native 12V-2x6 lead is not needed here. PowerColor's 750W minimum is met by 850W; no 1000W upgrade is justified.
- **Board slot:** MSI specifies the GPU-length slot **PCI_E2, PCIe 4.0 x16**. The physically upper PCI_E1 is x1. PCIe 5.0 GPU compatibility does not make this a Gen5 graphics slot. Use M2_1 for the PCIe 4.0 x4 2280 SN7100.
- **Clearance:** Freezer 36 is 159mm high versus H6's 163mm limit: **4mm nominal margin**. Use its supplied AM5 hardware and normal fan position; do not assume room for a raised fan. Low-profile FIT V is suitable on paper; physical fit/stability is not bench-tested. H6 permits 365mm GPU length and 200mm PSU length; compact Reaper and 140mm RM850e fit the published envelope. Verify side-panel cable routing during build.
- **Memory:** Two 16GB DIMMs in A2/B2. Boot at default JEDEC first, then test EXPO; 6000MT/s is an overclocked profile, not guaranteed stability. No exact QVL claim is made.
- **Case/cooling:** Original H6 includes three intake fans. Its 3-pin fans need appropriate DC control. No extra fans, AIO or RGB accessories are required in this eight-part launch bill. Freezer 36 includes thermal compound; do not add a separate paste purchase by default.
- **Trade-offs:** 1TB reduces initial cost but holds fewer large games than 2TB. RM850e has a seven-year manufacturer warranty rather than RM850x's ten-year position; approve that trade-off, not an assertion the units are identical. Air cooling simplifies assembly and servicing.

## Buying policy proposal
Keep **£1,750 delivered GOOD BUY** and **over £1,750 to £1,850 delivered ACCEPTABLE** as provisional commercial limits for the XT/32GB/1TB specification, requiring owner approval. Above £1,850 normally advise waiting or comparing the prebuilt. These are judgements anchored to the current £1,849.99 competitor, not claims that the DIY basket can be purchased at them. The current basket needs at least **£54.72 plus delivery** removed to reach acceptable, or **£154.72 plus delivery** to reach good buy.

“Acceptable” should mean acceptable for someone who wants to build and values explicit parts. It must not mean cheaper than a complete Windows prebuilt. Compare separately with the customer's legitimate OS requirement; do not assume a free transferable Windows licence.

Retain 24-hour maximum evidence age for positive badges; recheck exact seller, stock and final delivered basket immediately before activation and before customers order. A page retrieval alone does not refresh an approved offer. Missing delivery, contradictory identity/stock or absent owner approval prevents activation regardless of subtotal. Retain the existing 48-hour current-total hiding rule; an outdated comparison can remain visibly dated research only.

Review value bands weekly through launch, immediately after a material competitor or component price change, and daily during promotion periods. Do not loosen limits automatically after a normal short-term price rise. Component alerts should prompt review, not force approval: prioritise GPU and RAM reductions; retain existing live limits until a new revision is expressly approved. This phase does not pretend a fully justified independent ceiling for every component can be derived from one basket.
