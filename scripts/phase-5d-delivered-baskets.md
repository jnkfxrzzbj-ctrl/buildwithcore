## Compatibility resolved
- MSI's official CPU compatibility table associates Ryzen 5 9600X with **7E81v1A0**. The retail unit's installed version remains unknown. Document a BIOS check/update if required; do not continue calling the minimum version unknown. [MSI support](https://www.msi.com/Motherboard/B850M-GAMING-PLUS-WIFI6E/support).
- AM5 CPU/board/cooler; two DIMMs in A2/B2; boot JEDEC before enabling/test EXPO. No exact QVL or guaranteed 6000MT/s stability claim. GPU goes in PCI_E2 (PCIe 4.0 x16); SSD in M2_1. Follow the exact [MSI manual](https://download-2.msi.com/archive/mnu_exe/mb/B850MGAMINGPLUSWIFI6E_English.pdf).
- Reaper requires **two 8-pin PCIe** inputs; manufacturer minimum PSU is 750W. 850W is sufficient on specification. Use two separate supplied RM850x PCIe 6+2 leads; never EPS/CPU or modular cables from another PSU. The native 12V-2x6 lead is unused. [PowerColor](https://www.powercolor.com/product-detail212.htm); [Corsair cable list](https://www.corsair.com/uk/en/p/psu/CP-9020270-UK/rmx-series-rm850x-fully-modular-power-supply-uk-cp-9020270-uk).
- Freezer 36 is 159mm; H6 limit 163mm gives 4mm nominal margin. Use supplied AM5 mounting and normal fan position; do not assume space to raise a fan. Supplied thermal compound avoids an unnecessary extra purchase. [Arctic sheet](https://www.arctic.de/media/6b/50/57/1709195123/Spec_Sheet_Freezer_36_EN.pdf).
- Reaper's published envelope (289mm body / 304mm including bracket) is below 365mm case allowance. RM850x length 160mm is below the 200mm allowance. Original H6 includes three intake fans; set appropriate DC control for its 3-pin fans. [NZXT](https://support.nzxt.com/hc/en-us/articles/40183529624347-H6-Flow-2023-Specs).
- These are documentary compatibility checks, not a physical build, thermal or stability test. 1TB is an explicit capacity trade-off versus historical C's 2TB.
