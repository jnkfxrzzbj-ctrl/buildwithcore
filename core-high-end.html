Specification: RECOMMENDED

Reviewed 25 Oct 2026

Who it is for. An enthusiast building for 4K, ray tracing or CPU-limited high-refresh gaming, who accepts diminishing returns.

A distinct premium specification: RTX 5080 graphics, a gaming-focused 9800X3D and 2TB storage. Its price premium is most defensible for the intended ray-tracing and high-refresh audience, rather than maximum raster frames per pound.

Why these parts, and what to consider

Strengths

  • RTX 5080 provides a stronger ray-tracing proposition than the Performance tier.
  • 9800X3D targets CPU-limited gaming; 2TB gives more library space.
  • Stock-power air cooling and the specified native PSU cable avoid unnecessary liquid-cooling complexity.

Trade-offs

  • Both tiers have 16GB graphics memory. The price increase is not matched by proportional raster performance.
  • 4K ray tracing can still need upscaling and reduced settings; CPU gains shrink when GPU-limited.
  • The dual-tower cooler and GPU power cable need careful installation and clearance checks before closing the glass.

Premium specialist tier with diminishing returns. Performance remains the more balanced choice for most 1440p builders.

Phantom Spirit 120 EVO
